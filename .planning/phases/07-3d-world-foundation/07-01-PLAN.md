<objective>
Build the first 3D Coup sandbox with React Three Fiber: a canvas, placeholder table and seat cubes for 2–6 players, and a turn-following camera that glides around the table. No gameplay, and the 2D experience is not touched.

Purpose: Prove the R3F foundation (coordinate system, seat layout, camera motion) before the user's real low-poly GLB models (room, table, chair+person, cards, coins) are added in later phases.
Output: Route `/coup/3d` with a working 3D scene, debug HUD and 2D/3D toggle.
</objective>
